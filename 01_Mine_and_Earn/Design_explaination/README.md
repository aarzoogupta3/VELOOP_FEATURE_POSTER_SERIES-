01. Concept

Mine. Earn. Repeat.

The concept presents VEloop's Mine & Earn feature as a continuous digital reward experience where users stay active, build progress, and earn VE rewards through their activity.

02. Visual Direction

A futuristic digital reward environment using a dark tech-inspired background with gold and purple accents. The VE coin is treated as the central visual element, supported by floating reward assets, energy effects, progress indicators, and a gamified interface.

03. Feature Being Promoted

VEloop Mine & Earn

The poster promotes the feature that allows users to keep their earning session active and progress toward platform rewards.

04. Why the Visual Represents the Feature

The central VE coin represents the reward generated through the earning session, while the surrounding energy effects and floating reward elements communicate continuous activity and accumulation. The “Earning Session Active” interface and progress bar make the feature more tangible by showing an active earning state and progression toward rewards. The “Start Mining” CTA reinforces the action users are expected to take.
