Poster 02 — Design Explanation
Concept

The concept focuses on the idea of putting eligible credits to work for a defined period through VEloop's Stake & Earn feature.

The central idea was to represent staking as a controlled digital system, rather than presenting it like a traditional financial or investment advertisement. The futuristic vault acts as the main visual metaphor for credits being placed into the Stake & Earn mechanism.

Visual Direction

The visual direction continues the established VEloop Rewards campaign language from Poster 01, using a dark futuristic environment with gold and purple lighting, glowing reward elements, and digital interface components.

For this poster, the visual treatment is intentionally more structured and controlled than the energetic Mine & Earn poster. The central vault creates a strong focal point, while the surrounding circular structure and interface elements reinforce the idea of a defined digital process.

Feature Being Promoted

Stake & Earn

The poster communicates that eligible credits can be put to work for a defined period, with the applicable return presented as part of the feature rather than as a guaranteed financial outcome.

Why the Visual Represents the Feature

The central VE credit inside the digital vault represents eligible credits being committed to the Stake & Earn feature.

The surrounding structure creates a sense of containment and defined duration, while the Stake Details card provides a simple visual representation of the feature's key elements:

Eligible Credits
Period
Status
Applicable Return

The locked-credit indicator reinforces the idea of credits being committed for a specific period.

The headline “PUT YOUR CREDITS TO WORK” communicates the feature's purpose directly, while “EXPLORE THE FEATURE” works as a clear CTA without making unsupported claims about returns.

Campaign Consistency

The poster maintains continuity with Poster 01 through the same dark environment, gold/purple palette, reward visuals, typography treatment, glowing accents and digital interface language, while introducing a different visual concept appropriate to the Stake & Earn feature.
