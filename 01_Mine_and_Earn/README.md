# VEloop Rewards — Mine & Earn Poster

## Internship Design Assignment

A promotional poster designed for **VEloop Rewards** as part of the Graphhic Design Internship assignment.

The creative promotes VEloop's **Mine & Earn** feature and presents it as a futuristic, gamified digital rewards experience.

---

## Concept

### Mine. Earn. Repeat.

The concept presents VEloop's Mine & Earn feature as a continuous digital reward experience where users remain active, build progress, and earn VE rewards through their activity.

---

## Visual Direction

The poster uses a futuristic digital environment with:

- Dark technology-inspired surroundings
- Gold and purple lighting
- VE reward assets
- Energy and motion effects
- Gamified progress indicators
- Digital interface elements
- A prominent call-to-action

The visual language was designed to communicate an energetic, modern and reward-focused experience while avoiding a cryptocurrency or investment-oriented appearance.

---

## Feature Being Promoted

**VEloop Mine & Earn**

The poster promotes the Mine & Earn experience, encouraging users to keep their earning session active and progress toward rewards.

---

## Why the Visual Represents the Feature

The central VE coin represents the reward generated through the earning experience.

The surrounding energy effects and floating reward elements communicate continuous activity and accumulation, while the **Earning Session** interface and progress indicator visually represent active progress.

The **"Start Mining"** CTA reinforces the primary action associated with the feature.
