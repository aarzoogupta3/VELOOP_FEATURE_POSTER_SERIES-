# VEloop Rewards — Stake & Earn Poster

## Internship Design Assignment

A promotional poster designed for **VEloop Rewards** as part of a 5-poster marketing campaign during my internship.

This poster promotes the **Stake & Earn** feature and presents it as a structured digital rewards experience where eligible credits can be put to work for a defined period.

## Concept

### Put Your Credits to Work.

The concept presents the Stake & Earn feature as a controlled digital experience where eligible VE credits can be committed for a defined period and receive the applicable return.

The visual uses the idea of a futuristic digital vault to represent credits being placed into the staking system.

The overall message is:

**Eligible Credits → Defined Period → Applicable Return**

---

## Visual Direction

The poster was designed as part of the same visual campaign as the **Mine & Earn** poster.

The visual direction combines:

- Dark futuristic environments
- VEloop's gold and purple visual language
- Reward/VE coin elements
- Digital interface components
- Progress and status indicators
- Premium technology-inspired lighting
- Structured information cards
- Clear CTA-driven composition

While maintaining the same campaign identity, the visual language for this poster is intentionally more structured and controlled than the energetic Mine & Earn concept.

---

## Feature Being Promoted

**VEloop Stake & Earn**

The poster communicates the concept of putting eligible credits to work for a defined period and receiving the applicable return.

No specific return percentage or guaranteed financial outcome has been represented in the design.

---

## Why the Visual Represents the Feature

The central VE credit is placed inside a futuristic digital vault to visually represent credits being committed to the Stake & Earn system.

The surrounding circular structure represents the defined staking environment, while the interface card communicates important elements such as:

- Eligible credits
- Defined period
- Current status
- Applicable return

The locked-credit visual further reinforces the idea of credits being committed for a specified period.

The CTA **"Explore the Feature"** provides a direct action without making unsupported financial claims.

---

## Design Process

The poster was developed through multiple stages:

1. Understanding the provided Stake & Earn brief.
2. Developing the core visual concept around a digital credit vault.
3. Establishing a visual direction consistent with Poster 01.
4. Exploring and refining the background environment.
5. Creating and arranging the main VE credit visual.
6. Developing the supporting interface/stake details card.
7. Establishing typography hierarchy and campaign messaging.
8. Integrating supplied VEloop brand/reward assets.
9. Refining lighting, spacing, composition and visual balance.
10. Finalizing the poster for campaign consistency.

---

## AI-Assisted Visual Generation

The futuristic background environment used in this poster was generated with **ChatGPT's image-generation tool**.

The background was not used as a single unmodified generation. Multiple iterations were explored and refined to achieve the required:

- Composition
- Lighting
- Futuristic environment
- Gold and purple visual treatment
- Central focal area
- Negative space for typography
- Overall campaign consistency

AI image generation was used specifically as a **visual-generation aid for the background environment**.

The following creative decisions and execution were handled by the designer:

- Overall concept
- Creative direction
- Visual hierarchy
- Layout and composition
- Typography
- Copy placement
- Brand treatment
- Feature communication
- UI/stake information card
- Asset selection and placement
- CTA design
- Colour and lighting adjustments
- Final image composition
- Final poster refinement

The final poster is therefore a combination of **AI-assisted background generation and designer-led creative direction and execution**.
